Privacy Policy & User Data Protection

Last Updated: November 2026 • App: Sree Coir Broadcaster • Owner: Sree Coir Products

At Sree Coir Broadcaster ("we", "our", or "us"), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting personal information collected through our official WhatsApp Business Cloud API communication services, in accordance with applicable data protection laws including the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and Meta Platform Policies.

1. Purpose of Data Processing

We operate an opt-in communication service enabling wholesale coir buyers, distributors, and registered trade leads to receive:

  • Current daily and weekly wholesale rate quotations (e.g. Coir Pith 5kg Blocks, Curled Coir Rope, Geotextiles).
  • Order shipment notifications, packing lists, and export container status updates.
  • Product specifications, testing reports, and seasonal availability announcements.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect and process minimal data necessary to fulfill authorized communications:

  • Contact Information: Phone number (in standard E.164 format) and optional buyer or business name.
  • Consent Records: Timestamp of opt-in, method of consent (e.g., website inquiry, trade fair registration, or admin dashboard import), and active consent status.
  • Delivery Telemetry: WhatsApp message delivery statuses (sent, delivered, read, failed) received via official Meta webhooks to ensure message delivery reliability.

🛡️ Our Commitment: Zero Third-Party Data Sharing

We never sell, lease, or monetize customer phone numbers or contact data. Personal information is strictly utilized for direct B2B communication from Sree Coir Products via Meta's secure WhatsApp Business infrastructure.

3. Consent & Instant "STOP" Opt-Out Safeguards

We strictly uphold a consent-mandatory policy. We only send broadcast messages to individuals who have expressly opted in.

How to Opt-Out Anytime:

  • Any recipient may immediately opt out of receiving future messages by replying with the keyword "STOP", "UNSUBSCRIBE", or "CANCEL" in the WhatsApp chat.
  • Our automated webhook engine immediately detects this keyword and flags your profile as opted_out in real time.
  • Opted-out numbers are automatically suppressed and excluded from all future broadcast campaigns.

4. User Data Deletion Policy & Instructions

In compliance with Meta Platform Policies and personal data regulations, users have the absolute right to request complete deletion of their personal data from our systems.

🗑️ How to Request Complete Data Deletion

To permanently erase your phone number, name, and message history from our database, you may:

  • Reply with "DELETE MY DATA" directly in the WhatsApp conversation, OR
  • Send an email to our Data Protection Officer at: privacy@sreecoir.com (or contact: +91 83093 43915) with your registered phone number.

Upon verification, all associated subscriber records, contact tags, and message logs will be permanently purged within 48 business hours.

5. Data Security & Storage

All customer records are maintained on secured, private databases protected by industry-standard encryption, strict authentication controls, and tokenized session access. API access tokens and secret keys are stored in encrypted environments and never exposed to unauthorized parties.
